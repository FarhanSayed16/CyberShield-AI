# CyberSentinel

Employees already paste company data into ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini. CyberSentinel records who sent what, scores the risk, and can warn or block the prompt before the site sends it.

**What a buyer sees**

- Chrome extension on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Google Search
- Admin dashboard: activity, alerts, team, policies, billing
- Explainable URL, email, and prompt checks in the same console
- Image authenticity checks. Video analysis is not included

**Controls**

- Observe, warn, or block, chosen per organization. New installs observe only
- Manager can close AI tabs or pause AI pages on that employee’s browser
- Email on high and critical alerts, plus an on-demand daily digest

**Price**

Starter is ₹4,999 or $149 per month for up to 10 people. A 14-day trial is the default for a new organization.

**Not in this release**

SSO, a tokenization vault, on-device ML, shadow-AI coverage of unknown websites, a threat-intel marketplace, and a full SIEM.
